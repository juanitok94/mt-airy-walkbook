What is a Walkbook?

A Walkbook is a self-guided digital passport for a main street, neighborhood, or business district. Visitors collect stamps at each stop, unlock insider tips, and discover what makes a place worth walking.

No app to download. No account to create. Works on any phone, in any browser, even offline. The whole thing runs in localStorage — privacy-first by design.

For business owners, it's foot traffic driven by curiosity, not advertising. For visitors, it's the feeling of being an insider on day one.

How a Walkbook Gets Built

01
Curate the Stops
We walk the street, talk to owners, and pick 10 stops that define the character of the place. Quality, not comprehensiveness.
02
Write the Voice
Each stop gets a headline, a story, and an insider tip. The voice is the city's — not a review site, not a press release.
03
Build & Deploy
Mobile-first Next.js app. Deployed to Vercel. Works offline. No auth, no database, no app store submission. Live in days.
04
Hand to the Street
QR codes for each business. A shareable link. The passport lives on the visitor's phone. Word of mouth does the rest.
